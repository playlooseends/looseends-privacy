Loose Ends: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Loose Ends is a puzzle game. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking in the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, settings, daily streak) is saved on your device. We never receive it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app sends your total score to Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name or any other details.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks levels 101 to 300 is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game contains no ads, no links to social networks and no data collection, and is suitable for all ages.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page. Questions: support@example.com.
