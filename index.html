Loose Ends: Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This page covers the iPhone and iPad game Loose Ends: Connect Dots (“Loose Ends”). Loose Ends is a puzzle game. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking in the app, and the game works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, settings, daily streak) is saved on your device only. We never receive it. If you delete the app, your saved progress is deleted with it. Purchases are kept by Apple and can be restored.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app sends your total score to Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, your Apple ID or any other details.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks levels 101 to 300 is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Information from Apple

If you choose to share app usage or crash data with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect and TestFlight. These do not identify you.

Children

The game contains no ads, no links to social networks, no chat, no accounts and no data collection, and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

Support and contact

Questions, problems or feedback? Email looseends.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and, if it helps, the level number.

To restore a purchase, open the game, tap the gear icon (Settings), then tap Restore Purchases.
